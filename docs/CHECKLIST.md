# Before you post

## Facts
- [ ] Every claim in the captions matches a source you can name, and you've kept the links.
- [ ] Debated ideas are framed as debated ("may", "one idea is", "still debated").
- [ ] Animal-study results say which animal ("in mice").
- [ ] Numbers are the ones the sources give (an "estimated 430 million", not "half a billion").
- [ ] Nothing is overstated, and nobody's story is sensationalised.

## Picture (check `out/NAME_sheet.png`, then watch the video)
- [ ] Nothing important sits under the captions or near the bottom and right edges.
- [ ] Neighbouring areas have clearly different tones and don't melt together.
- [ ] Red appears only on villains.
- [ ] Labels don't cover faces or each other.
- [ ] Every caption has something on screen that shows what it says.
- [ ] There are no title or end cards, and the video fades out cleanly.

## Sound
- [ ] Every word is clear. Fix any odd pronunciations (see VOICE).
- [ ] The video doesn't drag where it slowed down to wait for the voice. If it does, shorten that line.
- [ ] No effect is louder than the voice.

## Posting text
Write one or two plain sentences, then exactly five hashtags with `#ai` among them:

> In 1922, a man in Nebraska fell while hanging a hog and started hiccuping. He didn't stop for 68 years. Here's what a hiccup actually is.
>
> #hiccups #biology #science #history #ai

Keep the source links ready in case someone asks.
