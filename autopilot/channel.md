# Channel brief

Claude reads this before every video. The part between the FOCUS markers is what the
"What should your videos be about?" box in the app changes. The rules below it always apply.

<!-- FOCUS START -->
## What this channel is about
Educational storytelling, with safety built in. Each video tells a short, true, gripping story or answers a question people actually wonder about, and the viewer walks away knowing something useful.

Mix three kinds of video:
1. How your body works: everyday mysteries explained plainly (what hiccups are, why we yawn, what goosebumps do), with a practical takeaway.
2. True stories from history and science, like Ötzi the Iceman, the 5,300-year-old man hikers found frozen in the Alps.
3. Staying safe: real risks explained calmly, like how people get scammed or approached by strangers on games like Roblox, how to spot it, and what to do (block, report, tell a trusted adult).

Audience: curious teens and adults.
<!-- FOCUS END -->

## Rules (always apply)
- Made with the Permeable Framework: narrator voice over everything, 1-bit cream-and-ink look, Bellhead character.
- Every fact must be accurate and checkable. Follow docs/CHECKLIST.md. Debated ideas are framed as debated.
- Safety topics: calm and practical, never scary for the sake of it. Give clear steps (stop, block, report,
  tell a trusted adult, call emergency services). Never show or describe how to do the harmful thing itself.
  Name real platforms only for well-documented, general risks, and keep it fair.
<!-- RATING START -->
- Content rating: **PG-13**. Scary, intense, dark or mature themes are fine, like a TV drama or a horror movie
  trailer, as long as it stays within TikTok and YouTube community guidelines. Nothing R-rated: no graphic gore
  or close-up injuries, no sexual content, no strong swearing, nothing that glorifies self-harm or real violence.
<!-- RATING END -->
- 35 to 70 seconds, 8 to 13 captions. Follow docs/STYLE.md. No title cards or end cards.
- Never repeat a topic or story part that's already in autopilot/history.jsonl. If a story series is running,
  continue it in order.
