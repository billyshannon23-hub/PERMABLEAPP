# Channel brief (edit me)

This is what the autopilot reads before every video. Change it to steer the channel.

## What the channel posts
- Short vertical videos made with the Permeable Framework: narrator voice over everything, 1-bit cream-and-ink look, Bellhead character.
- Two kinds of video, mixed through the week:
  1. **Story series**: second-person survival stories ("You were sitting in seat 23C when..."),
     such as the zombie outbreak on a plane, the zombie cruise ship, the zombie oil rig and the alien invasion.
     Each part ends on a cliffhanger and continues the previous part.
  2. **Science explainers**: one question a viewer would actually ask, answered plainly.
- Everything must be scientifically accurate. Survival details (water, food, injuries, physics) follow real facts.

## Rules
- 35 to 70 seconds, 8 to 13 captions.
- Follow docs/STYLE.md strictly. No title cards or end cards.
- Nothing gory or graphic. Tension comes from situations, not violence.
- Never repeat a topic or part that's already in autopilot/history.jsonl.
