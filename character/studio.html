DISPLAY

There are no arms: each hand is a floating ball, so a new pose is just a hand position. In side, three-quarter and back views the far hand is drawn behind the body and disappears where the body covers it. Your outfit is remembered in this browser.

MODEL SHEET

SHAPE

Head about a third of total height, a slightly squared circle. Neck pinches in, then a bell that widens to the base. No legs.

FACE

Two dot eyes with a fleck of light, short heavy brows that do most of the acting, a wide mouth with creases at the corners and a small chin line under it.

HANDS

No arms, just two round hands floating beside the body. They wobble with the same line as the body, rise and fall with its breath, and can hold things.

WARDROBE

Only two colours exist, so clothes are told apart by tone and pattern: grey t-shirt, dark hoodie, striped sweater, black suit, white lab coat, overalls.
